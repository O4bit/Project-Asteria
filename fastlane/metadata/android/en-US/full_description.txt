Project Asteria is a 100% Free and Open Source (FOSS) application for exploring the wonders of the universe. 

Discover the cosmos through:
- **Astronomy Picture of the Day (APOD)**: Daily high-quality images and explanations from NASA.
- **Space Facts**: Explore Near-Earth Objects (NEOs) and mission insights.
- **Privacy First**: No tracking, no ads, and 100% open source.
- **Modern UI**: Built with Jetpack Compose for a smooth and beautiful experience.
